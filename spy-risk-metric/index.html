How the model works

A weekly-only framework intended to measure long-term market position, not short-term direction.

200-Week Risk: Calculates ln(price ÷ 200W MA) from weekly closes, then ranks that deviation against its trailing 20-year weekly history.

Weekly Updates: Historical readings change at each weekly close and carry across intervening daily observations.

Percentile Normalization: Each reading uses only information available on that date, avoiding look-ahead bias.

Risk Prices: Prices are statistical scenarios using the current 200W average—not price targets.

Risk / VIX Alignment: Both comparison charts begin with reliable VIX history in 1990 and share the same date range.

Valuation-Aware Downside: Applies a 15× forward P/E scenario after explicit earnings shocks.
