Bitcoin Market-Cap-Adjusted Power-Law Fair Value

A maturity-adjusted long-run scenario extending Bitcoin's historical power-law path through 2040.

Long-run scenario, not a short-term price target. The path begins with Bitcoin's historical power-law regression, then progressively slows its excess growth as the modeled market cap approaches the selected gold-linked threshold. In the base case, at $20T the power-law growth above a 6% long-run nominal rate is reduced by half; the path continues converging toward 6% as the asset grows. The threshold grows 5.2% annually with the gold model, and the calculation assumes 20.8M BTC.geffective = g6% + (gpower-law − g6%) × 1 / [1 + (M / Kscenario,t)²]Here, M is the modeled fair-value market cap and Kscenario,t is the selected, annually growing damping threshold. The 6% mature-asset rate is informed by gold: free-floating gold compounded about 8% from 1971–2023, while the World Gold Council's long-term model estimates 5.2% annually for 2025–2040. Bitcoin retains more of its historical power-law growth while its market cap is smaller, then moves toward a gold-like mature growth rate. “Gap to Fair Value” compares each future value with today's BTC price; it is not a forecast return.

How combined risk works

Two independent views of Bitcoin's position, combined to balance long-term structure and cycle momentum.

Power-Law Regression: log₁₀(price) vs log₁₀(days since Jan 3 2009). Historical risk uses an as-of regression at each date to avoid future-price calibration.

Structural Risk: Normalizes the model residual against a time-decaying upper envelope fitted from cycle peaks.

Momentum Risk: A four-year rolling z-score mapped through a Gaussian CDF, adapting to declining volatility.

Combined: Geometric mean √(S × M). When either frame indicates low risk, the combined reading is pulled lower.

Halving Countdown: Uses current block height when available and estimates the next 210,000-block epoch at ten minutes per block.

Bear Market Progress: Uses the 363–376 day range of the two most recent completed bear markets, with the midpoint used only for the progress bar. The price zone spans the power-law lower boundary through the 0.46× trend level seen at the 2018 bottom. Because the 2025 ATH reached only 1.09× trend— versus 2.14× in 2021, 6.02× in 2017, and 8.42× in 2013—the base case allows capitulation to occur through time and apathy rather than requiring another blow-off decline. An external liquidity or leverage shock can still push price below the modeled zone.
