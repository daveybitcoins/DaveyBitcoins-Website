Strategy Rules (8/13/21 EMA)

Entry: Weekly 8 EMA crosses above 13 EMA while both are below the 21 EMA. This "early entry" catches the trend reversal before the full bullish stack forms.

Exit (Gap Mode): When the gap (21W − 8W) / 21W crosses above 5%, meaning the 8-week EMA has fallen far enough below the 21-week EMA to signal momentum loss.

Note: This is a backtest on weekly closes from historical data. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Signals may differ slightly from TradingView due to weekly candle alignment.
