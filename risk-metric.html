Bitcoin Market-Cap-Adjusted Power-Law Fair Value

Long-run scenario, not a short-term price target. The path begins with Bitcoin’s historical power-law regression, then progressively slows its excess growth as the modeled market cap approaches the selected gold-linked threshold. In the base case, at $23T the power-law growth above a 6% long-run nominal rate is reduced by half; the path continues converging toward 6% as the asset grows. The threshold grows 5.2% annually as a modeling assumption based on the gold-return estimate, and the calculation holds projected Bitcoin supply at 20.8M BTC. geffective = g6% + (gpower-law − g6%) × 1 / [1 + (M / Kscenario,t)²] Here, M is the modeled fair-value market cap and Kscenario,t is the selected, annually growing damping threshold. The 6% mature-asset rate is informed by gold: free-floating gold compounded about 8% from 1971–2023, while the World Gold Council’s long-term model estimates 5.2% annually for 2025–2040. Bitcoin retains more of its historical power-law growth while its market cap is smaller, then moves toward a gold-like mature growth rate. “Gap to Fair Value” compares each future value with today’s BTC price; it is not a forecast return.

Methodology (Combined Risk)

Power-Law Regression: log₁₀(price) vs log₁₀(days since Jan 3 2009). The smooth chart line uses the fixed full-history curve. Readings begin after a 30-observation warm-up, then historical risk uses an as-of regression at each date so past readings are not calibrated with future prices.

Structural Risk (Decaying Envelope): Normalizes the residual against a time-decaying upper envelope fitted from cycle peaks, with a continuous 0.005 floor. The max deviation capacity shrinks as Bitcoin matures.

Momentum Risk (Rolling Z-Score → CDF): 4-year rolling z-score mapped through Gaussian CDF. Cycle-sensitive, adapts to declining volatility.

Combined: Geometric mean √(S × M) of both. Zones are Accumulate 0.00–0.20, Neutral 0.20–0.50, Caution 0.50–0.80, and Euphoria 0.80–1.00 everywhere on the site.

Halving Countdown: Uses the current Bitcoin block height when available, targets the next 210,000-block subsidy epoch, and estimates the date at 10 minutes per block. If the height API is unavailable, it falls back to a clock-based estimate from block 840,000.

Bear Market Progress: Starts from the latest all-time high, compares elapsed days with the average duration of Bitcoin's four completed bear markets, and estimates a downside range from the model's lower boundary on the average-duration target date to the lowest close since the current cycle high.

Updates: The source price dataset is refreshed daily by automation. On page load, the page fetches a current BTC quote and recalculates every card, chart, table, and projection as one coherent snapshot. Use the snapshot refresh control for a new quote; the price is never updated independently from its dependent metrics. Tabs left open reload after a new UTC day begins.
