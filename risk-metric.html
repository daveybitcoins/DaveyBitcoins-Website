Bitcoin Market-Cap-Damped Power-Law Fair Value

Long-run scenario, not a short-term price target. The path begins with Bitcoin’s historical power-law regression, then progressively slows its excess growth as the modeled market cap approaches $20T. At $20T, half of the growth above a 10% long-run nominal rate is damped; the path continues converging toward 10% as the asset grows. The calculation assumes 20.8M BTC. “Gap to Fair Value” compares each future value with today’s BTC price; it is not a forecast return.

Methodology (Combined Risk)

Power-Law Regression: log₁₀(price) vs log₁₀(days since Jan 3 2009). The smooth chart line uses the fixed full-history curve. Historical risk uses an as-of regression at each date so past risk readings are not calibrated with future prices.

Structural Risk (Decaying Envelope): Normalizes the residual against a time-decaying upper envelope fitted from cycle peaks. The max deviation capacity shrinks as Bitcoin matures, producing more realistic risk boundaries.

Momentum Risk (Rolling Z-Score → CDF): 4-year rolling z-score mapped through Gaussian CDF. Cycle-sensitive, adapts to declining volatility.

Combined: Geometric mean √(S × M) of both. Pulls toward the lower signal — if either frame says low risk, that's more informative. Historical low tables use a calibrated floor-break score when structural risk falls below the lower model boundary, so deep-cycle lows are not displayed as literal zero risk.

Halving Countdown: Uses the current Bitcoin block height when available, targets the next 210,000-block subsidy epoch, and estimates the date at 10 minutes per block. If the height API is unavailable, it falls back to a clock-based estimate from block 840,000.

Bear Market Progress: Starts from the latest all-time high, compares elapsed days with the average duration of completed Bitcoin bear markets, and estimates a downside range from the model's 0.00 risk floor on the average-duration target date to the lowest close since the current cycle high.

Updates: The source price dataset is refreshed daily by automation. On page load, the page also fetches the current BTC price from live quote APIs and appends it to the in-memory dataset, so every dashboard card, chart, table, and projection is recalculated from the latest available reading. Tabs left open reload after a new UTC day begins.
